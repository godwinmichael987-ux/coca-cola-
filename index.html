coca cola

Coca-Cola was created in 1886 by Dr. John S. Pemberton, a pharmacist in Atlanta, Georgia, USA. Pemberton originally developed the drink as a medicinal beverage and sold it at a pharmacy. It was intended to be a refreshing tonic, and its early marketing claimed it could help with problems such as fatigue and headaches. The drink was mixed with carbonated water and served as a fountain beverage. Frank M. Robinson, Pemberton's bookkeeper, came up with the name “Coca-Cola” and designed its famous script lettering. The drink eventually moved from being marketed as a medicinal tonic to becoming a popular soft drink sold around the world. In short: Coca-Cola started in 1886 as a pharmacist's flavored, carbonated beverage that was partly marketed for medicinal/tonic purposes, but it eventually became a mainstream soft drink.
