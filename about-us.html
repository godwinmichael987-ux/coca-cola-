About us

Story

We are The Coca‑Cola Company, a brand born in 1886 in Atlanta, Georgia. What started as a single refreshing drink has grown into one of the most recognized and beloved beverages in the world. For more than a century, we’ve been sharing moments of happiness, bringing people together, and creating experiences that transcend borders.

Global presence

From bustling cities to quiet villages, our beverages are enjoyed in over 200 countries. We don’t just make Coca‑Cola — our portfolio includes sparkling soft drinks, juices, waters, teas, coffees, and sports drinks. Every sip represents our commitment to refresh the world and make a difference.

purpose

We exist to refresh the world and make a positive impact. That means: Sustainability: Reducing our carbon footprint, conserving water, and designing packaging that supports a circular economy. Community: Supporting local projects, empowering women, and investing in education. Innovation: Creating new flavors, healthier options, and exciting experiences for future generations.

promise

We believe in the power of connection. Whether it’s a family gathering, a celebration, or a quiet moment alone, Coca‑Cola is there to add a spark of joy. Our promise is simple: to deliver refreshment, optimism, and togetherness in every bottle and can.

coca cola

Coca-Cola was created in 1886 by Dr. John S. Pemberton, a pharmacist in Atlanta, Georgia, USA. Pemberton originally developed the drink as a medicinal beverage and sold it at a pharmacy. It was intended to be a refreshing tonic, and its early marketing claimed it could help with problems such as fatigue and headaches. The drink was mixed with carbonated water and served as a fountain beverage. Frank M. Robinson, Pemberton's bookkeeper, came up with the name “Coca-Cola” and designed its famous script lettering. The drink eventually moved from being marketed as a medicinal tonic to becoming a popular soft drink sold around the world. In short: Coca-Cola started in 1886 as a pharmacist's flavored, carbonated beverage that was partly marketed for medicinal/tonic purposes, but it eventually became a mainstream soft drink.
